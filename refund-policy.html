Refunds & Cancellations

Cheakstar sells digital products — ebooks and digital learning material — through our store on Payhip. Digital products are delivered instantly after payment, so our refund rules are simple and specific.

When we refund in full

  • Duplicate payment — you were charged twice for the same product.
  • Wrong product delivered — the file you received is not the product you paid for, and we cannot supply the correct one.
  • File does not work — the download is corrupt or unreadable and we cannot provide a working copy after you contact us.
  • Payment taken, nothing delivered — the payment succeeded but no download was made available to you.

When we cannot refund

  • Change of mind after the product has been downloaded.
  • The product was bought by mistake and has already been downloaded.
  • Compatibility issues that the product page clearly describes in advance (for example, the required file format is stated on the page).

How to ask

Email us from the same email address used for the purchase, within 14 days of buying, at rohitkrchandoliya@gmail.com (or through the Contact page). Tell us the product name and the order email. If the refund applies, we process it through the same payment method used for the purchase, through our store provider Payhip, within 7 working days of approving the request.

Training and services

Free workshops carry no payment and no refund question. Paid training or security-check services, when offered, are booked separately in writing, and the cancellation terms given in that written agreement apply.
