About the company

Cheakstar Private Limited is a cybersecurity company based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. We work in three areas: practical cybersecurity books and digital products, hands-on training for students and beginners, and authorized website security checks for small businesses. This website also offers 58 free browser-based tools, a learning blog, and free video content published under the ByteShield name. Our rule in everything: simple language, permission first, and honesty over hype — no income promises and no inflated claims, ever.

The Founder

Rohit Kumar Chandoliya is the Founder and Managing Director of Cheakstar Private Limited. A cybersecurity researcher and author from Jaipur, he has written the Secure The Future book series and works in bug bounty — finding and reporting security issues, with permission, on public programs. Cheakstar is the company through which his books, training and security work reach the people they can help.
