Website Security Check

A careful, plain-language security check of your own website — what is weak, how serious it is, and exactly how to fix it.

Written authorization only. We test only websites whose owner has authorized the check in writing, and only within the agreed scope. We never touch any website without its owner's permission — this is a hard rule, with no exceptions.

What You Get

  • A written report in simple language — no jargon walls
  • Verified findings only — every issue confirmed before it is reported
  • Severity and impact for each finding, honestly rated
  • Step-by-step fixes your developer can follow

How It Works

  • 1. You contact us and tell us about your website
  • 2. We agree the scope, and you give written authorization
  • 3. We check carefully and gently — your site stays live and safe
  • 4. You receive the report, and we explain every finding on a call or chat

Who It Is For

Small businesses, online shops, coaching institutes, agencies and anyone whose website matters to their work — and who wants one honest check before a problem finds them first.

Pricing depends on the size of your website and is agreed with you in writing before any work begins.
