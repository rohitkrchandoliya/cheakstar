What's New

The latest changes to this website, newest first — in plain words, with no hype. This page is updated whenever something meaningful ships.

November 2026

97 free tools. The tools hub now carries 97 practical tools that run in your browser. New arrivals include a currency converter, an internet speed test, a typing speed test, Markdown preview, a code formatter, a regex tester, a timestamp converter, a JWT decoder and a colour palette generator — free, private, and with nothing uploaded to our servers.

New help and company pages. This What's New page itself, plus a FAQ, a Press & Media Kit, an Accessibility statement and a human-readable Sitemap — so answers and company facts are easier to find.

Book detail pages and a complete catalogue. The flagship books — the Secure The Future series, Bug Bounty from Scratch and The Honest Report — now have their own detail pages, and the Books & Store page lists the full catalogue of 25 titles.

Devices & sessions. Settings gained a Devices & sessions view: see the devices signed in to your account and sign out the ones you no longer use — including a sign-out-everywhere option for safety.

Sign in with Google. Alongside email sign-in, you can now sign in to your Cheakstar account with Google. Two-factor authentication remains available in Settings for extra protection.

Earlier in November 2026

58 free tools, live. The tools hub now carries 58 practical tools that run in your browser — PDF, image, audio and video work, text utilities, security and OSINT lookups, calculators and more — free, with no uploads to our servers for file tools.

Accounts, reward points and a leaderboard. Free accounts arrived with reward points for genuine active time on the site, favourite and recent tools on your account page, appearance choices (light, dark or system theme, text size, reduced motion) that follow you across the site, and a public leaderboard for members who choose to appear on it.

October 2026

The Cheakstar Blog. Our blog launched with practical, plainly written cybersecurity guides — password habits, phishing, updates, bug bounty and more — with new articles published regularly since. Start from the Blog page.
