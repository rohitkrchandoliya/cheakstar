Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Cheakstar Private Limited, a company registered in India (CIN: U85500RJ2023PTC090736), based in Jaipur, Rajasthan. On this site we publish cybersecurity books and learning content, offer training and authorised website security checks, and provide free online tools and games. This policy explains, in plain words, what information the website collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Accounts. You may create a free account on this website. Sign-in is handled by Supabase, our authentication provider: your email address and password are processed by Supabase, and your password is never stored on this website itself. On your account we store your email address, your display name, and the settings and shortcuts that make the site work for you — your favourite and recently used tools, and your appearance preferences. If you add them, your mobile number and profile photo are stored on your account too. If you turn on two-factor authentication, that is also processed by Supabase.

Reward points. Signed-in members earn reward points for genuine active time on the site. We store your points total, the points earned each day, your rank, whether you choose to appear on the public leaderboard, and — for the leaderboard only — a small copy of your profile photo. Only your display name, profile photo, points and rank ever appear on the leaderboard, and only while your "show on leaderboard" setting is on — never email addresses or other private details. If you browse as a guest, your active time is kept only in your own browser and joins your account as points when you sign in.

Stored in your browser. The site saves small settings in your browser's local storage so it behaves the way you left it: your theme (light, dark or system), text size and motion preferences, sidebar layout, checklist ticks, and similar display choices. This information stays on your device, is not sent to us, and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

The free tools. Almost every tool on this site works entirely inside your browser: files you open with a tool are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to us. A few lookup tools work differently by their nature — for example the breach checks, DNS and domain lookups, IP lookups and the security headers check send the address or identifier you type in to the third-party data provider that holds the answer, so the result can be fetched for you. Do not enter information into a lookup tool that you are not comfortable sharing with the relevant provider.

Email sign-ups. If you ask for the free chapter and checklist by email, we use your name and email address only to send you those resources and occasional Cheakstar emails about new books and resources. You can ask us to stop and delete your details at any time by replying to any email.

When you contact us. If you message us — through the contact form on this website, LinkedIn or our store — we receive what you choose to send and use it only to answer you. Messages sent through the website form are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms (see the providers section below).

Cookies and advertising

This website shows advertising served by Google AdSense, and a cookie consent message is shown to visitors where the law requires one. Please read this part carefully:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Essential browser storage (described above) is used only to make the site work as you set it up; it is not used by us for advertising.

Who processes information for us

We use a small number of service providers to run this website: GitHub Pages (website hosting), Supabase (accounts, sign-in and account data), Brevo (sending our emails), Web3Forms (delivering messages sent through our contact form to our inbox) and Google (advertising, and forms where we use them). Each provider processes information only to provide its service. We do not sell your personal information to anyone, and we never share it for someone else's marketing.

Your rights and choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it — including deleting your account and its stored details completely. Email our Grievance Officer using the contact details below and we will act on your request. You can also change most account details yourself in Settings, hide your name from the leaderboard there, and clear the settings stored in your browser at any time.

How long we keep information

Account information is kept while your account exists, and is deleted when you ask us to delete the account. Email sign-up details are kept until you ask us to stop or delete them. Settings stored in your browser stay on your device until you clear them.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS, account sign-in is handled by Supabase, and two-factor authentication is available for accounts. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care with the information entrusted to us.

Children

This website offers general educational content and is not directed at children under 13. Accounts and purchases are intended for adults, or for minors acting with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a fresh "last updated" date. Continuing to use the website after a change means the updated policy applies.
