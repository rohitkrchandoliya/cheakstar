Accessibility Statement

We want this website to be usable by everyone. This page states, honestly and only, what the site does today — and how to tell us when something falls short.

What the site does today

Semantic structure. Pages are built with proper landmarks — header, navigation, main sections and footer — and a single, ordered heading structure, so screen readers and other assistive tools can move through a page the way it was meant to be read.

Keyboard-reachable controls. Links, buttons and form fields are standard HTML controls: they can be reached with the Tab key and operated from the keyboard, and the focused control is shown with a visible gold outline. In the site sidebar, Escape closes the menu and Ctrl/Cmd+B toggles it.

Appearance choices. In Settings you can choose a light, dark or system theme, a larger text size, and reduced motion. Your choices are remembered on your device, survive sign-out, and apply across the whole site, tools included — content is written to stay readable in both the light and the dark theme.

Plain language. We write in simple, correct English on purpose: if a beginner cannot understand a page, we rewrite it. Clear language is an accessibility feature too.

Labelled controls. Form fields on this site carry text labels, and the tools use labelled inputs, so controls can be identified by name rather than by position or colour alone.

What we do not claim

We are a small team, and this site has not been audited or certified against any accessibility standard — so we make no such claim here. Some parts of the site, especially the tools, are still improving, and some may be harder to use with assistive technology than they should be.

Tell us what is hard to use

If any part of this website is difficult for you to use, please tell us through the Contact page or by emailing our Grievance Officer, Rohit Kumar Chandoliya, at rohitkrchandoliya@gmail.com. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we will try to fix it — and, where we can, find another way to get you the same content in the meantime.
