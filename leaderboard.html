How points work

  • 1 active minute = 1 point. Reading, learning, using tools and playing games all count.
  • Only genuine activity counts — the tab in front of you, with real activity on the page.
  • Idle time never counts, and only one tab earns at a time.
  • There is no daily limit — every active minute earns its point, even if you stay all day.
  • Points never expire, and your level never goes down.
  • Points have no cash value — they cannot be bought, sold, exchanged or withdrawn. They are recognition of your learning time, and nothing more.
  • The public board shows only your display name, points, level and photo — and a photo appears only while you keep "show on the leaderboard" switched on. You can hide yourself at any time in My Account or Settings.
  • Time you spend here as a guest is kept in your browser and joins your account as points when you sign in.
