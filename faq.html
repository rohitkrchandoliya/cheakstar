Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about this website, in plain words. If your question is not here, ask us through the Contact page — we reply to every genuine message.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. All 58 tools on this site are free to use, and every tool itself opens and works without an account. A few moments ask for a free login — revealing a tool's result, reading the rest of a blog article, or sending us a message — because accounts are how we keep the site sustainable and personal. The account is also free, forever, and no card is ever needed.

Do my files get uploaded when I use a tool?

No. Almost every tool works entirely inside your browser: the file you open is processed on your own device and is not uploaded to us. A few lookup tools are different by their nature — breach checks, DNS and domain lookups, IP lookups and the security headers check send the address or identifier you type to the third-party data provider that holds the answer. Do not enter anything into a lookup tool that you are not comfortable sharing with that provider. Our Privacy Policy explains this in full.

Why does the site ask me to log in at some moments?

The site stays fully browsable while signed out. The free login appears only at "value moments" — a tool's result, the rest of an article, sending a message — and it unlocks your account features: favourite and recent tools, appearance settings that follow you, and reward points. If the sign-in service itself is ever unreachable, the site simply stays open; you are never locked out by an outage on our side.

What are reward points?

Signed-in members earn points for genuine active time on the site — learning and using tools, not leaving a tab open. Points recognise that time and place you on the public leaderboard if you choose to appear there. Points have no cash value: they cannot be bought, sold, exchanged or withdrawn, and ranks never go down. The full rules are in our Terms of Use.

How do I buy a book, and how is it delivered?

Our books are sold through our official store on Payhip — the Books & Store page lists every title and links to the store. All titles are digital PDF editions: after payment, your download link arrives from Payhip right away. See the store for current prices and offers. If a download ever goes wrong, message us through the store or LinkedIn and we will sort it out.

What about refunds?

Purchases are covered by the store's checkout terms and our Refund & Cancellation policy. In short and in plain words: if something goes wrong with an order — a failed download, the wrong file, a duplicate payment — contact us through the store with your order details and we will put it right. For anything else, write to us first through the Contact page and we will answer honestly.

Can I read something before buying?

Yes — a lot. Chapter 1 of Bug Bounty from Scratch is free (from the store, or by email through the Books Readers list on the Books & Store page), the Blog and Free Resources are free, and our ByteShield videos teach the same subjects for free.

Do you offer training?

Yes — workshops and courses for students and beginners, announced on the Training page when places open. Training starts from zero and focuses on skills you can use, practised legally and safely.

Can you check my website's security?

Yes — a plain-language security check with verified findings and clear fixes, described on the Security Services page. One rule never changes: a check begins only after the scope is agreed and you have given written authorization for your own website. We never test a website without its owner's permission.

How do I change my details, or delete my account?

Most details — display name, appearance, leaderboard visibility — can be changed in Settings. To delete your account and its stored details completely, email our Grievance Officer, Rohit Kumar Chandoliya, at rohitkrchandoliya@gmail.com, and we will act on your request. The Privacy Policy lists your rights in full.

Is my account protected?

Sign-in is handled by Supabase, our authentication provider, and two-factor authentication is available in Settings — we recommend turning it on. You can also review the devices signed in to your account, and sign out any of them, from the Devices & sessions view in Settings.
