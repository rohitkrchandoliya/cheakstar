Bug Bounty from Scratch

Your first 90 days as a security researcher — a day-by-day path from setup and legal practice to your first well-written report. Written by Rohit Kumar Chandoliya in simple English. Digital PDF edition, sold and delivered through our official store on Payhip. See the store for current prices and offers.

Who this book is for

  • Beginners starting bug bounty who want one honest order to learn in, instead of scattered videos and tips
  • Students who understand basic web concepts and want to move into real, legal practice
  • New hunters who have not yet written a report — or whose first reports were not accepted

The path inside

  • Foundations first: how the web works — HTTP requests and responses, cookies and sessions, how browsers and servers talk
  • One vulnerability class at a time: understand a single class deeply — what it is, why it happens, how to prove it, how to fix it
  • Legal practice: free labs and training ranges where mistakes are welcome — never systems outside a programme's scope
  • Read real reports: what strong, accepted submissions look like — clear steps, honest impact, repeatable evidence
  • Join a programme and start small: read the scope and rules twice, test only what is listed, and write like a professional from your first report
