The Honest Report

How to write bug bounty reports that get accepted — clear structure, honest severity, real evidence, and practical fixes. Written by Rohit Kumar Chandoliya in simple English. Digital PDF edition, sold and delivered through our official store on Payhip. See the store for current prices and offers.

Who this book is for

  • New hunters preparing their first submissions
  • Hunters whose reports were rejected as unclear, unproven or overstated — and who want to fix the writing, not just the finding
  • Any researcher who wants a professional reporting habit: a report an engineer can reproduce in five minutes

What is inside

  • Clear structure: the parts every accepted report has, in the order triage teams read them
  • Honest severity: how to state impact truthfully — strong findings do not need exaggeration
  • Real evidence: exact steps and proof that make a finding repeatable by someone who was not there
  • Practical fixes: how to suggest a remedy the engineering team can actually apply
A companion for your desk: the Bug Bounty Report Template Pack in our store holds six professional report templates — IDOR, XSS, authentication/session, information disclosure, SQL injection and SSRF — ready to adapt to your own findings.
