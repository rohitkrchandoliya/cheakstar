About Cheakstar

Cheakstar Private Limited is a cybersecurity company based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, India. We work in three areas: practical cybersecurity books and digital products, hands-on training for students and beginners, and authorized website security checks for small businesses.

Company Facts

  • Legal name: Cheakstar Private Limited
  • Based in: Jaipur, Rajasthan, India
  • Founded: 2023
  • Founder: Rohit Kumar Chandoliya
  • Registered: Private limited company, GST registered

What We Believe

  • Simple language wins — if a beginner cannot understand it, we rewrite it
  • Permission first — security skills are practised only where authorized
  • Honesty over hype — no income promises, no inflated claims, ever
  • Learn, practise, grow — skill comes from doing, in the right order

The Founder

Rohit Kumar Chandoliya is a cybersecurity researcher and author from Jaipur. He has written the Secure The Future book series and works in bug bounty — finding and reporting security issues, with permission, on public programs. Cheakstar is the company through which his books, training and security work reach the people they can help.
