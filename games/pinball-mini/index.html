🕹️ Pinball Mini

Hold Space, the down arrow, or the Launch button to charge the plunger, then release to launch. Flippers: left and right arrow keys, Z and M, or tap the left and right halves of the table. Bumpers score 100, rollovers 50, pegs 10. Light all three rollovers to complete the mission for a 500-point bonus, and land a launch in the gold skill band for a 250-point skill-shot chance. The ball-save lamp protects the first seconds of every ball.

Press Start. Hold Space, the down arrow, or the Launch button to charge the plunger, then let go to launch.

Keys: ArrowLeft / Z = left flipper, ArrowRight / M = right flipper, hold Space or ArrowDown to charge the plunger and release to launch, A and D nudge the ball (three hard nudges inside two seconds and the table TILTS - flippers dead and no scoring for three seconds), P pauses, R starts a new game at once. Touch: tap and hold the left or right half of the table to flip that side - the halves are marked FLIP on the table itself.

High scores (this device)

    Your progress - high scores with your initials, best score and games played - is saved in this browser on this device, and when you are signed in it also syncs privately to your Cheakstar account, so it follows you across your devices. Honest note: the physics here is a simplified home-grown model - circles, line segments and fixed-step integration - not a full pinball simulation, so the ball can occasionally behave unlike a real table.
