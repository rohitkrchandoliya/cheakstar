Whack-a-Bug

A 45-second round. Bugs pop out of the server racks - tap them before they hide. A normal bug scores 10 points and a gold bug 30, and every five hits in a row without a miss or an escape raise your combo multiplier, up to x5 - hits score more while the combo lasts. The bugs get faster as the round goes on, and near the end three can be up at once. How many can you catch?

Keys 1-9 whack, P pauses.

Press Start round, then tap the bugs as they pop up.

Your privacy: this game runs fully on your device. When you are signed in, your progress also syncs privately to your Cheakstar account, so it follows you across your devices; it is never shared or used for tracking. Your best score and the most bugs you have caught in a single round are saved in this browser on this device when a round ends, so they are still here when you come back. You can erase everything at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Honest note: the bugs are cartoon software bugs popping out of server racks - no real systems are involved.
