BlockFit Puzzle

Place the three pieces on the 10 by 10 board. Tap a piece to pick it, then tap a board square to place it - or drag a piece onto the board. A full row or column clears, and clearing two or more at once scores combo points. Every piece has its own colour, and the squares it fills keep that colour. Keyboard: press 1, 2 or 3 to pick a piece, move the gold cursor with the arrow keys, and press Enter to place; U takes back your last placement. The game ends when no piece fits anywhere. Reaching 500, 1,000 and 2,000 points earns milestone badges.
