Word Finder

Find the hidden tech and security words in the letter grid: 6 words on Easy (8 by 8), 8 on Normal (10 by 10) or 12 on Hard (12 by 12). Drag across a word, tap its first and last letters, or play with the keyboard: click the grid, move with the arrow keys, and press Enter to mark the start and the end of a word. The timer starts on your first selection; your best time for each difficulty is saved on this device.
