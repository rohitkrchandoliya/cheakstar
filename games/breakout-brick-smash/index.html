Breakout Brick Smash

Move the paddle with your mouse, your finger, the left and right arrow keys (or A and D), or by holding the on-screen arrow buttons. Click the board or press Space to launch the ball, and P pauses. Smash every brick to clear the level - each new level brings a new wall and a faster ball. Catch the falling capsules: W widens the paddle for 12 seconds, M splits the ball into three, S slows the ball for 10 seconds. You have 3 lives.
