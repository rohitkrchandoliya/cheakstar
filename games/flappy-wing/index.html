Flappy Wing

Click, tap or press Space to flap. Fly through the gaps in the pipes - one point for every pipe you pass. Earn a medal at 10, 20, 30 and 40 points; the pipes creep closer together and faster as your score climbs. Press P to pause. Touch a pipe or the ground and the run ends.
