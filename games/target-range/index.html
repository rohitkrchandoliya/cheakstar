Target Range

A 45-second round on the range. Targets pop up - click or tap them before they vanish; the closer to the centre, the more you score: bullseye 50, inner ring 25, outer ring 10. Choose Round 1, 2 or 3 before you start: higher rounds have smaller, shorter-lived targets, and Round 3 targets drift. Press P or the Pause button to freeze the clock mid-round. Your best score and best accuracy are saved on this device.

Your privacy: this game runs fully on your device.
