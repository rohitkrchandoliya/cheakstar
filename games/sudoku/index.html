Sudoku

Fill every row, column and 3 by 3 box with the digits 1 to 9. Pick a difficulty, tap a cell, then tap a number. Wrong placements count as mistakes - three mistakes end the game. Use Notes mode to pencil in candidates, spend a Hint when you are stuck, and try the Daily Challenge: one puzzle, the same for everyone, every day.
