Space Blaster

A vertical scrolling space shooter: clear enemy waves, collect power-ups and beat the boss every fifth wave. Move with the arrow keys or A/D, drag on the board, or hold the on-screen arrows; P pauses, and your cannon fires on its own. Power-ups: F upgrades your cannon, S is a shield that absorbs one hit, L is an extra life.

Your privacy: Space Blaster runs fully on your device. Your best score, highest wave, most boss kills in one game and games played are saved in this browser on this device, so you can chase your own records, and when you are signed in they also sync privately to your Cheakstar account, so they follow you across your devices. Your progress is never shared or used for tracking, and your browser's site-data controls can erase everything at any time. Space Blaster draws on a canvas with pre-rendered ship sprites; enemies and bullets are reused from fixed pools, and collisions run through a broad-phase grid, so the action stays smooth.
