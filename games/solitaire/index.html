Solitaire

Build the four foundations up by suit from ace to king. On the table, build down in alternating colours; only kings fill empty columns. Click the stock to draw one card (unlimited passes), click a card to pick it up and click a destination to place it, or double-click a top card to send it home.

Click the stock to draw. Click a card to pick it up, then click where it should go. Double-click a top card to send it to a foundation.

Your privacy: this game runs fully on your device. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or tracked anywhere. Closing the page ends the game. Honest note: controls are click-to-move (there is no drag and drop), and deals come from a built-in set of shuffles that a solver program verified as winnable before release - the set cycles, so a deal you finish will come round again.
