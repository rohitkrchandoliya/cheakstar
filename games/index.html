Honest notes: all games here are original implementations built for this site. Ludo is the traditional board game; it plays on one device with pass-and-play or computer opponents - there is no online multiplayer. The Ludo computer opponent plays a fair casual game, not an expert one. The arcade games are original takes on classic ideas; Traffic Dodger is a top-down dodging game, not a driving simulator. Turbo Lane Racer is a pseudo-3D canvas racer (a segmented road in the style of classic arcade racers), not a real 3D game, and the Connect Four computer opponent is a casual player, not a perfect solver. Wave 3 notes: Pinball Mini uses simplified physics rather than a full pinball simulation. Chess Puzzles is a set of composed mate puzzles checked by a small built-in move checker - it is not a full chess engine or a complete game of chess (the puzzles never need castling or en passant, and pawns promote to queens only). Solitaire uses click-to-move controls (no drag and drop) and deals from a built-in set of shuffles that a solver verified as winnable before release. Parking Master and Paper Plane Pilot are simple 2D simulations, not 3D games.
