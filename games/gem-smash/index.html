Gem Smash

Swap two neighbouring gems to line up 3 or more of the same kind in a row or a column. Matched gems clear, new ones drop in, and chains score cascade bonuses. Reach the level target before your moves run out to clear the level and unlock the next. From level 4 some gems are frozen in ice: they cannot be swapped and do not match, but matching gems next to a frozen one breaks the ice (worth 20 bonus points each) and frees it. Play by tapping two gems, dragging one gem into its neighbour, or with the arrow keys plus Enter or Space. Every gem kind has its own colour, shape and letter, so kinds stay apart even without colour vision.
