Archery Master

Aim with the mouse or your finger, hold the press to draw the bow, and release to loose. The wind reading changes after every arrow and pushes it sideways. Round 1 the target stands still; in round 2 it moves. Rings score 50, 25, 15 and 10.

Your privacy: this game runs fully on your device. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or tracked anywhere. Closing the page ends the game. Honest note: arrow flight uses a simple gravity-and-wind model, and the target is scored by where the arrow crosses its plane - a friendly simplification of real archery.
