🏹 Archery Master

Aim with the mouse or your finger, hold the press to draw the bow, and release to loose. The wind reading changes after every arrow and pushes it sideways - watch the flag on the range. Round 1 the target stands still; in round 2 it moves; in round 3 it moves faster, the wind blows harder and the target stands farther away. Rings score 50, 25, 15 and 10.

Press Start. Move the mouse or finger to aim, hold the press to draw the bow, and let go to loose the arrow.

Keys: hold Space to draw and release to loose; ArrowUp and ArrowDown nudge the aim, the mouse wheel trims it finely, P pauses, R starts a new game at once. Touch: drag to aim, press and hold to draw, release to loose.

Your progress - best score, bullseyes, games played and hit rate - is saved in this browser on this device, and when you are signed in it also syncs privately to your Cheakstar account, so it follows you across your devices. It is never shared or used for tracking. Honest note: arrow flight uses a simple gravity-and-wind model, and the target is scored by where the arrow crosses its plane - a friendly simplification of real archery.
