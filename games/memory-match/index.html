Memory Match

Flip two cards at a time and find every matching pair. The cards use security words: locks, shields, keys, and more. Fewer moves and less time makes a better game. Choose Easy (6 pairs on a 4 × 3 grid), Normal (8 pairs on a 4 × 4 grid) or Hard (15 pairs on a 6 × 5 grid), and play by tapping or clicking cards, or with the arrow keys plus Enter or Space.
