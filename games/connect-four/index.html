Connect Four

Drop discs into the columns and be the first to line up four in a row: against the computer on three difficulty levels, or with a friend on the same device. Click a column button, click any cell in a column, or use the arrow keys and Enter, or the number keys 1 to 7. The disc you are about to drop is previewed above the board. Your tallies and win streak are saved on this device.

Your privacy: this game runs fully on your device. When you are signed in, your progress also syncs privately to your Cheakstar account, so it follows you across your devices; it is never shared or used for tracking. Your tallies against the computer and in two-player games, your win rate and your win streaks are saved in this browser on this device, so they are still here when you come back. You can erase everything at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The computer opponent has three levels: Easy plays casually but still takes a win when it can and blocks yours, Medium is the classic casual player (it takes wins, blocks yours and otherwise favours the centre), and Hard looks a few moves ahead before choosing. None of them is a perfect solver, so a careful player can still win. In two-player mode both players share this device.
