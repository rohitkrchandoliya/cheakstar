Connect Four

Drop discs into the columns and be the first to line up four in a row: against the computer on three difficulty levels, or with a friend on the same device. Click a column button, click any cell in a column, or use the arrow keys and Enter, or the number keys 1 to 7. The disc you are about to drop is previewed above the board. Your tallies and win streak are saved on this device.

Your privacy: this game runs fully on your device and nothing is uploaded or tracked. Your tallies against the computer and in two-player games, your win rate and your win streaks are saved only in this browser on this device, so they are still here when you come back; nothing is sent to Cheakstar or anyone else. You can erase everything at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The computer opponent has three levels: Easy plays casually but still takes a win when it can and blocks yours, Medium is the classic casual player (it takes wins, blocks yours and otherwise favours the centre), and Hard looks a few moves ahead before choosing. None of them is a perfect solver, so a careful player can still win. In two-player mode both players share this device.
