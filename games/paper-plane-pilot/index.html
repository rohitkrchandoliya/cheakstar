Your progress - best distance, best score, stars and badges - is saved in this browser on this device, and when you are signed in it also syncs privately to your Cheakstar account, so it follows you across your devices. It is never shared or used for tracking. Honest note: a tiny arcade glide model - real paper planes are at the mercy of far stranger physics than this.
