Guess the hidden cybersecurity and tech word letter by letter before the gallows is complete.

Tap a letter to make your first guess.

Type A to Z on your keyboard to guess a letter. Press Enter for a new game when a round ends. A hint reveals one letter but costs one wrong guess, and it switches off when only one wrong guess remains.

Your privacy: this game runs fully on your device. When you are signed in, your progress also syncs privately to your Cheakstar account, so it follows you across your devices; it is never shared or used for tracking. Your words solved, wins, streaks and your category and difficulty choices are saved in this browser on this device, so they are still here when you come back; you can erase everything at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Hangman picks each word from a built-in list of cybersecurity, networking and everyday tech terms, and shows the word's meaning when the game ends.
