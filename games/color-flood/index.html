Color Flood

Flood the whole board to a single colour within the move limit, starting from the top-left corner.

Moves: 0/25Size: Classic 14 x 14

Pick a colour to flood the board, starting from the top-left corner.

Your privacy: this game runs fully on your device. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or tracked anywhere. Closing the page ends the game. Color Flood deals a fresh random board each game and counts your moves honestly against the limit shown. The moves you need depend on the board you are dealt, so some boards finish with room to spare and others are tight.
