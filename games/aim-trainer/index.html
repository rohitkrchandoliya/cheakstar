🎯 Aim Trainer

Reaction: press Start, wait for the board to turn green, and click - or press Space - as fast as you can; clicking early is a fault, and five rounds give your average and best time. Precision: 30 seconds of targets that fade after about a second, with hits, accuracy and streaks tracked. Gridshot: the same targets for 60 seconds - every hit respawns one at once. Target size and speed apply to Precision and Gridshot.
