Classic Ludo

The traditional race board game. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base. Race all 4 tokens around the board and into the centre. Landing on an opponent sends their token back to base, unless the square is a safe square (marked with a star). Rolling a 6, capturing, or finishing a token gives another turn. Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
