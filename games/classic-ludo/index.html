Classic Ludo

The traditional race board game, rebuilt with rolling dice and step-by-step token hops. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base. Race all 4 tokens around the board and into the centre. Landing on an opponent sends their token back to base, unless the square is a safe square (marked with a star - all 8 of them). Rolling a 6, capturing, or finishing a token gives another turn. Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
