Sliding Puzzle

Slide the numbered tiles into order: 3 by 3, 4 by 4 or 5 by 5 boards.

Moves: 0Board: 4 x 4

Click a tile next to the blank space to slide it.

Your privacy: this game runs fully on your device. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or tracked anywhere. Closing the page ends the game. Sliding Puzzle shuffles by making random legal slides from the solved board, so every board it deals can always be solved. Your move count is kept only in this page.
