Sliding Puzzle

Slide the tiles into order on a 3 by 3, 4 by 4 or 5 by 5 board, in Numbers mode or in Picture mode, where the tiles rebuild one picture. Click or tap a tile next to the blank space, use the arrow keys or WASD, or swipe on the board. The timer starts on your first move; your best time and fewest moves for each board size are saved on this device.

Your privacy: this game runs fully on your device. When you are signed in, your progress also syncs privately to your Cheakstar account, so it follows you across your devices; it is never shared or used for tracking. Your best time, fewest moves, solve counts and solve streaks are saved in this browser on this device, so they are still here when you come back. You can erase everything at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Sliding Puzzle shuffles by making random legal slides from the solved board, so every board it deals can always be solved.
