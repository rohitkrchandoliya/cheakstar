Traffic Dodger

Steer your gold car with the left and right arrow keys, A and D, the on-screen hold buttons, or by dragging on the road. Press P to pause. Traffic keeps coming faster - pass close to a car without touching it for a 25-point near-miss bonus, and drive over a gold coin for 50 points. Every 500 points the level rises and the traffic gets denser.
