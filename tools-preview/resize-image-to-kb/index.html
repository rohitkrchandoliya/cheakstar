Resize Image to KB

Shrink an image to an exact target size in KB, for online form uploads.

Made for online forms: set the exact file size a form allows — for a photo or a signature upload — and this tool shrinks your image to at or under that size.

How it works: the tool first lowers the quality step by step. If even the lowest quality is still too big, it also makes the picture itself smaller, step by step, until the file fits. A very small target on a big photo can look soft — that is the real trade-off, and the result line always tells you the final size and dimensions.

Privacy: your image is processed only in this browser tab and is never sent to any server.
