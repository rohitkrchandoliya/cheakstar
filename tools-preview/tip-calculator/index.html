Tip Calculator

Work out the tip and split the bill fairly between friends.

Enter the bill, choose a tip percentage, and say how many people are sharing. You get the tip, the total, and exactly what each person pays — handy after a group dinner or a team lunch.

Quick tip:

Good to know: tipping customs differ by place and by type of service, and in some restaurants a service charge is already added to the bill — if it is, an extra tip is optional. This calculator simply does the arithmetic on the numbers you give it; the amounts are worked out on this device only.
