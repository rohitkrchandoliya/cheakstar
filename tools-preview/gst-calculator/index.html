GST Calculator

Add GST to a price, or take GST out of an inclusive price.

For bills, quotations and invoices: pick a standard GST slab or a custom rate, and see the CGST/SGST or IGST split.

Note: within a state, the GST amount is half CGST and half SGST; between states it is fully IGST. This calculator shows the split both ways. Rates follow the standard GST slabs — check the current rate for your item before filing.
