Rotate PDF

Rotate all pages, or chosen pages, of a PDF by 90, 180 or 270 degrees.

Fix sideways or upside-down pages — every page, or only the pages you list.

Your file stays on your device. Rotation runs entirely in your browser using pdf-lib (MIT licence). The original file is never changed — you download a new, rotated PDF. Pages are rotated relative to their current angle, so a page that is already rotated gets the new angle added.
