Harmonies

Complementary — the colour opposite on the wheel.

Analogous — neighbours, 30 degrees apart.

Triadic — three colours evenly spaced at 120 degrees.

Split-complementary — the two colours beside the opposite.

Tetradic — four colours at 90-degree steps.

CSS export

Good to know: shades, tints and tones are mixed in sRGB in 10% steps (the last step of each scale is pure black, white or mid-grey). Harmonies rotate the hue in OKLCH at the base colour's lightness and chroma; if a rotated colour would fall outside the sRGB gamut, its chroma is reduced just enough to fit. The name is the nearest of the 148 CSS colour names by a weighted (redmean) distance — a close neighbour, not an official name. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is sent anywhere.
