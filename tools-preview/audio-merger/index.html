Audio Merger

Join two or more audio files into one MP3, in your chosen order.

Honest limits, so there are no surprises: the media engine (ffmpeg) runs inside your browser and loads once (about 20–31 MB from a public CDN, then it is cached). The combined size of all files should stay under about 50 MB; any single file over 100 MB is refused. The engine is a single-thread build, so merging is slower than a desktop app. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.
