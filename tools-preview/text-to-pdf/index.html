Text to PDF

Type or paste text and download it as a clean PDF.

Type or paste plain text and turn it into a clean, properly paginated PDF — for notes, letters and simple documents.

Note: the PDF uses the standard Helvetica font, which suits plain English text; special symbols and emoji may not render. Long lines wrap automatically and long documents flow onto extra pages. Everything runs in your browser (jsPDF, MIT licence); nothing is uploaded.
