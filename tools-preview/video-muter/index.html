Download muted video

Choose or drop a video file to mute it.

Good to know — please read, honestly: browsers cannot rewrite a video file's container to simply delete its audio track, so this tool re-encodes the picture while it plays (in real time — a 10-minute video takes about 10 minutes) and records no audio at all. That means the output quality depends on your browser's encoder and is not bit-identical to the original picture, and the output format is whatever your browser records — MP4 in some browsers, WebM in others; the download name and the panel above always say which. If you need the original picture stream kept bit-for-bit with only the audio removed, use a desktop tool with a stream-copy option (for example ffmpeg with -c:v copy -an). Nothing is uploaded either way — this all happens in your tab.
