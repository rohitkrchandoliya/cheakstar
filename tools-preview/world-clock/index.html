World Clock

Live local time for major cities around the world.

The current time in major cities, updated every second and worked out from the official time zone rules for each place — daylight saving changes are handled automatically.

Good to know: offsets shown are each city's current difference from UTC (GMT). Many places move their clocks forward in summer and back in winter, so the offset — and the gap between two cities — can change during the year. The clocks use your device clock as their source; if your device time is wrong, every clock here will be off by the same amount.
