EMI Calculator

Monthly instalment, total interest and total payment for any loan.

For a home, car, personal or education loan: enter the loan amount, the interest rate and the time period.

Note: this uses the standard reducing-balance EMI formula with monthly rests, the way most Indian banks calculate. Your bank's actual EMI can differ slightly because of fees, insurance, or the exact start date. Figures are estimates for planning, not a loan offer.
