Loan Amortization Calculator

See your loan balance fall month by month, with interest and principal for every payment.

An EMI tells you what you pay each month — this schedule shows where that money goes. Early payments are mostly interest; later ones mostly principal. Enter any home, car or personal loan to see the full month-by-month table.

Note: the schedule uses the standard reducing-balance method with monthly rests, rounding each month to the nearest paisa; the final payment is adjusted so the balance ends at exactly zero. Your bank's statement can differ slightly because of fees, insurance premiums, or the exact date the first EMI falls. Figures are estimates for planning, not a loan offer.
