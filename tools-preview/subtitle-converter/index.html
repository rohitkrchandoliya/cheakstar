Subtitle Converter

Convert subtitles between SRT, WebVTT and ASS — with time shifting and rescaling — right in your browser.

Paste subtitle text below or drop a subtitle file, choose the output format, optionally shift every cue earlier or later, or rescale all timings (handy for frame-rate changes: multiply by 23.976/25, for example). Conversion is exact text processing on this device — nothing is uploaded.

    Good to know — please read: styling is not carried between formats: ASS override tags (colours, positions, karaoke) are removed with a warning, and SRT/VTT carry plain text only, so a conversion through this tool keeps the words and the timings, not the styling. WebVTT NOTE and STYLE blocks are skipped and counted in the warnings. Cues that a negative shift would push before 00:00:00 are clamped to zero and counted. Everything runs locally in this tab.
