URL Encoder & Decoder

Encode or decode URL (percent-encoded) text safely.

URLs cannot contain spaces and many special characters, so they are stored in percent-encoded form (a space becomes %20).

Which mode? "Part of a URL" encodes everything special, including / and ?, so the result can sit safely inside a link. "Full URL" leaves the structure of a complete link (https://, /, ?, &) untouched and only encodes the unsafe characters. Your text never leaves this browser tab.
