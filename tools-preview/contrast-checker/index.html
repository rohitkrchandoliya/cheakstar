Contrast Checker

Check WCAG text contrast between two colours — AA and AAA for normal and large text, with one-click fixes.

Choose a text colour and a background colour. The ratio and the WCAG pass/fail badges update live, and if the pair fails AA for normal text, the tool works out the smallest change to the text colour that passes.

Normal text — body copy at 16px. The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Readable text is not a luxury: visitors with low vision depend on contrast like this.

Good to know: ratios follow the WCAG 2.1 formula, (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05) from relative luminance. "Large text" means at least 24px, or 18.66px and bold. The fixes adjust only the text colour, mixing it toward black or white in sRGB until AA normal (4.5 : 1) is just reached — always re-check the fixed pair in your real design, because font weight and size change how much contrast you truly need. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is sent anywhere.
