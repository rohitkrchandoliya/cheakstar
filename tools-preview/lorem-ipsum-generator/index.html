Lorem Ipsum Generator

Generate placeholder text for designs, drafts and mock-ups.

Make dummy text in seconds — paragraphs for a page layout, sentences for a heading, single words for a logo mock-up, or a list for a menu draft. The text is assembled on this device from a built-in word bank, so it works offline.

Good to know: placeholder text has no meaning on purpose — it shows how a layout looks before the real words are written, and it should be replaced before anything is published. The classic opening sentence is the traditional dummy text used by printers and designers for centuries. Everything is generated in this browser tab and nothing is sent anywhere.
