BMR & Calorie Calculator

Work out your daily calorie needs from your body and activity level.

Your BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the energy your body burns at complete rest. Multiply it by your activity level and you get your TDEE — the calories you burn in a normal day. This calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, the one most dietitians start from.

Honest note: BMR formulas are population estimates — two people with the same age, weight and height can differ by 10% or more. The "lose" and "gain" targets use the common 500 kcal rule of thumb; steady, moderate changes are safer than extreme diets, and very low calorie intakes should only be followed with medical supervision.
