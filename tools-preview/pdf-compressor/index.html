PDF Compressor

Make a PDF smaller by re-rendering its pages as compressed images.

Good for scanned documents and PDFs that a form refuses because of size.

Honest note: this method turns every page into an image, so the text in the compressed PDF is a picture — it can no longer be selected, copied or searched, and at the smallest setting text can look soft. If your PDF is already mostly text, it may not shrink much (it can even grow). Everything runs in your browser (PDF.js by Mozilla + jsPDF); nothing is uploaded.
