Phone Number Checker

Check a phone number's country, line type, formats and calling links.

Enter any phone number with its country code to see real facts about it: the country, the kind of line (mobile, landline, toll-free and more), its standard formats, one-tap call and WhatsApp links, and whether the number is even possible.

Number details

What this cannot do: no free tool or database can tell you who owns a number or its live carrier company — that data is private and held by the telecom operators. Anyone selling "owner name from number" lookups is guessing or using leaked data. This tool reports only what the number itself proves: its country, line type and formats, checked on this device.
