Scientific Calculator

A full scientific calculator with trig, logs, powers and memory.

Tap the keys or use your keyboard. Trigonometry starts in degrees — use the DEG key to switch to radians. The expression is evaluated by a built-in parser on this device; nothing is sent anywhere.

0

Good to know: "%" turns the number before it into a percentage (50% = 0.5), the way phone calculators treat it. Factorial (n!) works for whole numbers up to 170. Results follow the DEG/RAD setting shown on the keypad. Long expressions are capped at 200 characters.
