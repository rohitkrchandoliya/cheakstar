Compound Interest Calculator

See how a lump sum grows with periodic compounding, year by year.

Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest as well. Enter an amount, a rate and a time period to see the growth, with an optional monthly deposit on top.

Honest note: this assumes one steady rate for the whole period and a deposit at the start of each month. Real products change rates over time, and fees or tax can reduce what you actually receive. Treat the figures as a planning estimate, not a promise.
