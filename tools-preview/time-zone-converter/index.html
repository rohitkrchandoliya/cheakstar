Time Zone Converter

Convert a date and time between any two time zones.

Planning a call, a class or a deadline across countries? Enter the date and time in one city and see the same moment in another, with the exact time difference between them.

Good to know: conversions use the official time zone rules for each city, including daylight saving on the date you pick — so a meeting time converted for January can differ by an hour from the same clock time converted for July. Times are read from the date and time you enter; nothing is sent anywhere.
