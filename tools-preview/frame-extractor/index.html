Frame Extractor

Pull still images out of a video — one moment, every few seconds, or a whole range — plus a contact sheet.

Drop a video, then click a moment on the timeline strip or choose a mode below: a single time, every N seconds, or a range. Frames come out at the video's full size as PNG or JPEG, individually or zipped. Everything happens on this device — your video never leaves this browser tab.

Choose or drop a video file to extract its frames.

Good to know — please read: frames are the pictures your browser decodes from the video, saved at the video's full size — if the source is blurry, the stills will be too. A batch is capped at 200 frames so the tab does not run out of memory, and the status line says so if your settings would have made more. The ZIP download uses the JSZip library loaded from a CDN; if it cannot load (for example offline), the ZIP button stays disabled and each frame can still be downloaded on its own. Nothing is uploaded.
