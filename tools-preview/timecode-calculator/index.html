Good to know — please read: drop-frame timecode does not drop any actual frames — it skips frame numbers (two numbers at the start of most minutes at 29.97, four at 59.94) so the clock stays in step with real time. At 29.97 drop-frame, one hour of timecode is 107,892 frames, not 108,000. "Real time" is the true playback duration: frame count divided by the exact rate (for example 30000/1001 for 29.97). Non-drop 23.976 counts frames at 24, as editors do.
