Image Resizer & Cropper

Resize an image to exact pixels, or crop it to an aspect ratio, with passport presets.

Start from a size preset — including two passport-photo sizes — or enter exact pixels, then crop to a fixed aspect ratio or free-crop.

Resizing to a larger size than the original will not add detail; it only stretches the image. Passport sizes here use 300 dpi equivalents (2 x 2 inch = 600 x 600 px, 35 x 45 mm = 413 x 531 px). Always check the official photo rules of the form or authority you are submitting to.

Privacy: your image is processed only in this browser tab and is never sent to any server.
