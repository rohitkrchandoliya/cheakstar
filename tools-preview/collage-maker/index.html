Photo Collage Maker

Combine your photos into one grid collage.

Pick 2 to 9 photos and this tool arranges them into one neat grid collage you can download as a PNG or JPEG. Each photo is centre-cropped to fill its cell evenly, and the collage is exported at 1200 pixels wide so it stays sharp when shared.

Good to know: your photos are processed on your device only — they are never uploaded or sent anywhere, and nothing is stored. Photos are centre-cropped to fill square cells, so the edges of wide or tall photos are trimmed; the spacing and background colour frame the grid. Very large photos are scaled down to fit the 1200-pixel-wide export.
