Photo Collage Maker

Combine your photos into one grid collage.

Pick 2 to 9 photos and this tool arranges them into one neat grid collage you can download as a PNG or JPEG. Each photo is centre-cropped to fill its cell evenly, and the collage is exported at 1200 pixels wide so it stays sharp when shared.

Good to know: your photos are processed on your device only — they are never uploaded or sent anywhere, and nothing is stored. In the grid layouts, photos are centre-cropped to fill square cells, so the edges of wide or tall photos are trimmed. The strip layouts never crop: every photo is shown whole, scaled to a shared width (vertical) or height (horizontal, up to 4000 px wide overall). In base-image mode the first photo is the base and the rest sit as a tile band over its bottom edge. Very large photos are scaled down to fit the export.
