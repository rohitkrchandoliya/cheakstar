Color Palette Generator

Build harmonious colour palettes from one base colour.

Pick a colour and choose a harmony — complementary, analogous, triadic and more. You get five colours that work together, each with a HEX code you can copy with one click.

Click any swatch to copy its HEX code.

Good to know: harmonies are worked out from the colour wheel — complementary pairs sit opposite each other, analogous colours sit side by side, and triadic colours sit 120 degrees apart. A pretty palette is not a guarantee of readable text: always check that text on a colour has enough contrast before using it for headings or buttons. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is sent anywhere.
