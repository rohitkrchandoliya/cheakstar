Pick a colour above, or choose an image to simulate it.

Good to know: simulations use the standard Vienot, Brettel and Mollon colour-vision matrices, applied per pixel in sRGB — a good approximation, not a medical measurement; two people with the same diagnosis still see differently. Never rely on colour alone: pair colours with labels, patterns, icons or position, so a chart or a status light still makes sense when two colours merge. Images are capped at 1600px on the longest side for speed. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is sent anywhere.
