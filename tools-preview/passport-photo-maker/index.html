Passport Photo Maker

Crop a photo to 35 x 45 mm and download it, plus a 4 x 6 inch sheet of 8 copies.

Upload a photo, position the face inside the 35 x 45 mm frame, then download the finished passport photo — and, if you want prints, a 4 x 6 inch sheet with 8 copies ready for a photo shop.

Sizes use 300 dpi: the photo itself is 413 x 531 pixels (35 x 45 mm), and the sheet is 1800 x 1200 pixels (6 x 4 inches). Drag the photo to move it, and use the corners of the frame to zoom. Always check the official photo rules of the form or authority you are submitting to — background colour, glasses and head-covering rules differ.

Privacy: your photo is processed only in this browser tab and is never sent to any server.
