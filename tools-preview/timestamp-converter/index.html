Unix Timestamp Converter

Turn Unix timestamps into human dates — and dates back into timestamps.

A Unix timestamp counts seconds since 1 January 1970 (the "epoch"). For example, 1700000000 is 14 November 2023, 22:13:20 UTC, and 0 is the epoch itself. This tool converts both ways, in seconds or milliseconds.

Good to know: auto-detect treats values of 1,000,000,000,000 or more as milliseconds and smaller values as seconds — the usual split, since current timestamps in seconds are about 1.7 billion and in milliseconds about 1.7 trillion. Dates before 1970 give negative timestamps, which are valid. Everything is calculated in this browser tab; nothing is sent anywhere.
