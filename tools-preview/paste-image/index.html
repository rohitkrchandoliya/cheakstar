Paste Image

Press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V) anywhere on this page to paste an image from your clipboard — then crop it and download it as PNG, JPEG or WebP.

Screenshots and copied images live on your clipboard, not in a file. Paste one here, trim it with the crop box, and save it — no upload, no sign-up. The image is handled on this device only and never leaves this browser tab.

Press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V to paste, or use a button:

The “Paste from clipboard” button needs your browser's permission; if it is unavailable, Ctrl+V always works.

Waiting for an image — press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V.

Good to know: the crop numbers are in the image's real pixels, not screen pixels — the readouts stay exact even when a large screenshot is shown scaled down. Drag the gold box to move the crop and the corner dot to resize it; with an aspect lock on, resizing keeps that shape. JPEG output is composited on white, because JPEG has no transparency. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is sent anywhere.
