BMI Calculator

Check your Body Mass Index from your height and weight, with the WHO category.

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a quick screening number based on your height and weight. Enter yours below, in metric or imperial units.

Honest note: BMI is a screening guide, not a diagnosis. It does not measure body fat directly, and it can mislead for athletes with high muscle mass, older adults, and during pregnancy. For a personal health judgement, speak to a doctor.
