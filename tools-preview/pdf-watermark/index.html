Add Watermark to PDF

Stamp text on every page of a PDF, on your device.

Choose a PDF, type your watermark text, and this tool stamps it on every page — diagonally across the centre, as a header, or as a footer. A light, see-through stamp is the usual choice for drafts and confidential documents.

Download watermarked PDF
Good to know: your PDF is processed on your device only, using the open-source pdf-lib library (MIT licence) — the file is never uploaded or sent anywhere. The watermark is drawn onto every page of a new copy; your original file is not changed. Password-protected or damaged PDFs cannot be stamped, and the tool will say so plainly.
