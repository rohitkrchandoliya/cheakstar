Screen Recorder

Record your screen, with your microphone mixed in if you want it, and download the video. Every take is kept in a list.

Useful for tutorials, demos and bug reports. The recording is made only in this browser tab; nothing is uploaded.

With mixing on, your microphone and the screen's own sound are blended into one audio track — tick "Share audio" in the browser's picker if you want the screen sound included. With mixing off, only the screen sound is kept, and only if you share it. Every finished recording is added to the takes list under the player, so you can record several takes and download the best one. Takes live in this tab only and are lost when it closes.

Ready. Your browser will ask which screen or window to share when you start.

Notes: mixing uses your browser’s audio tools to blend the microphone and the shared screen sound into a single track before recording; nothing is processed anywhere else. Screen recording works on desktop browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) and most Android browsers; iPhone Safari does not offer screen capture to websites — use the phone's own screen recorder there. Recordings are WebM files, which play in browsers and most players; use the Video Converter tool here if you need MP3 audio or other formats. If you stop sharing from the browser's own "Stop sharing" bar, the recording ends and is saved here the same way.
