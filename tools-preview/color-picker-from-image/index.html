Color Picker from Image

Click any point of an image to get the exact HEX and RGB colour there.

Pick the exact colour from any point of an image — for matching a brand colour, a background, or a design element. Click (or tap) the image where the colour is, hover to inspect single pixels through the zoom loupe, and read the image’s main colours as an extracted palette.

Zoom loupe

Under the pointer: Move the pointer over the image.

The loupe magnifies the pixels around the pointer, fifteen times over, with the exact centre pixel outlined. Click to pick that colour, just as before.

Shares are worked out by sampling the image and grouping near-identical colours together, so a compressed photo gives a close estimate rather than an exact census. Click any palette colour to load it as the picked colour.
