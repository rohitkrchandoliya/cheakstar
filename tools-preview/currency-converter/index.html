Currency Converter

Convert between world currencies using the daily reference rate.

Convert an amount from one currency to another — dollars to rupees, euros to pounds, and 30 currencies in all. The rate comes from the European Central Bank daily reference rate, fetched live when you convert.

Loading the latest reference rate…

Good to know: the rate shown is the European Central Bank daily reference rate for the date printed with the result — it is a daily reference rate, not a live trading rate, and banks and money-transfer services add their own fee or margin, so the amount you actually receive can differ. If the rate cannot be loaded (for example, you are offline), the page says so plainly and shows no number — a rate is never guessed or invented. Your amount is sent only to the public rates service to fetch the conversion; no account or personal detail is involved.
