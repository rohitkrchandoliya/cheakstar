RD Calculator

Estimate the maturity value of a monthly recurring deposit.

A recurring deposit builds a lump sum from a fixed monthly payment. Banks and post offices compound the interest every quarter — enter your monthly amount, the rate and the tenure to estimate the maturity value.

Honest note: this uses the standard bank formula, which assumes every monthly deposit is paid on time and the rate stays the same for the whole tenure. A missed or late instalment usually attracts a small penalty, and interest is taxable. The figure is an estimate, not a promise from any bank.
