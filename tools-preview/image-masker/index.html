Image Masker

Cut an image into a shape — circle, squircle, star, heart and more, or your own SVG shape — and download it as a transparent PNG, ready for profile pictures, stickers and design work.

Pick a shape, then zoom and drag the image until it sits exactly where you want it inside the shape. Everything outside the shape becomes transparent. Your image is processed on this device only and never leaves this browser tab.

Shape

The first <path> is used (a <rect>, <circle>, <ellipse> or <polygon> also works). Holes and multi-part paths follow the SVG fill rule of the path itself.

Drag the image to position it. The chequerboard is transparency — it is not part of the download.

Choose or drop an image to mask it.

Good to know: the output size follows the shape — round shapes (circle, hexagon, star, heart, triangle) are square at the image's smaller side, up to 2048 × 2048 px; the full frame, rounded rectangle and squircle keep the image's own proportions, capped at 2048 px on the longest side; a custom SVG shape is rendered at 1024 px on its longest side, following the SVG's own proportions. Very large photos are scaled to those caps, never upscaled. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is sent anywhere.
