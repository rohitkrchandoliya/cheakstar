Photo Privacy Checker (EXIF)

See what a photo secretly carries: GPS location, camera model and date taken.

Photos quietly carry extra data called EXIF: the camera or phone model, the date and time, and often the exact GPS location where the photo was taken. Pick a photo to see what it reveals before you share it.

Privacy: your photo is read only inside your browser, on this device. It is never uploaded to any server, including Cheakstar. Tip: most messaging apps strip some of this data when you send a photo as a message, but sending the original file (as a document or by email) usually keeps all of it.
