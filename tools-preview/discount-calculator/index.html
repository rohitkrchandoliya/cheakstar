Discount Calculator

Find the final price and your saving after one or two discounts.

Sale offers often stack two discounts ("50% + extra 20% off"), which is not the same as 70% off. Enter the price and the discounts to see what you will actually pay.

How stacked discounts work: the second discount applies to the already-reduced price, not the original. So 50% + 20% off means you pay 50% of the price, then 80% of that — a total saving of 60%, not 70%. The GST breakup, when selected, simply splits the final price into its pre-tax value and the tax part.
