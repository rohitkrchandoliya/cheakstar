Convert CGPA to percentage and percentage to CGPA.

Many forms ask for your marks as a percentage when your marksheet shows a CGPA — or the other way round. Pick the direction, choose the multiplier your board or university uses, and convert.

Honest note: there is no single national conversion formula. CBSE uses x 9.5; some universities use x 10, others use their own formula (for example, percentage = CGPA x 10 - 7.5 in a few cases). Always check the conversion printed on your marksheet or your university's rules before using the result on an official form.
