Audio and Video Tools

Convert audio between common formats, trim audio and video, pull the audio out of a video, make a short GIF, or convert short clips between MP4 and WebM. Everything runs in your browser. No account or sign-in is needed.

Please read before you convert: honest limits.
  • Best for short clips. Files up to about 50 MB work best; files over 100 MB are refused on this page.
  • The conversion engine (ffmpeg.wasm, single-threaded core) is about 20 to 31 MB and downloads once from the jsDelivr CDN the first time you convert. It is cached by your browser after that.
  • Conversion runs at roughly 10 to 20 percent of native speed, because it runs inside the browser. A 30 second clip can take a minute or more.
  • This page uses the single-threaded core on purpose. GitHub Pages cannot set the COOP and COEP headers that the multi-threaded core needs, so the faster multi-threaded core cannot run here.
  • Weak phones may struggle with video, and very long videos may run out of browser memory. For long videos, use a desktop app instead.
Your privacy. Your files never leave this device. Conversion happens entirely in your browser. The only thing downloaded from the internet is the conversion engine itself, from the public jsDelivr CDN. Nothing you convert is uploaded or stored anywhere.

1. Audio Converter

2. Video Tools (small files)

Work on short video clips: pull the audio out as MP3, trim a section, make a GIF, or convert between MP4 and WebM. The same honest limits above apply, and video is slower than audio.

Trim times only apply to the trim operation. The GIF uses the first 10 seconds of the video at most.
