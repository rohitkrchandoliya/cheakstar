PPF Calculator

Project your Public Provident Fund balance over 15 years and beyond.

The Public Provident Fund (PPF) is a government-backed savings scheme. Enter a yearly deposit and see the balance grow year by year at the interest rate you set.

Honest note: the PPF rate is set by the government and can change every quarter — 7.1% is the long-running recent rate, and the field is editable so you can test others. The projection follows the real PPF rule: interest is worked out month by month on the balance and credited once a year, and it assumes deposits reach the account by the 5th of the month. Deposits are limited to ₹1,50,000 per financial year. A PPF account matures in 15 years and can then be extended in 5-year blocks.
