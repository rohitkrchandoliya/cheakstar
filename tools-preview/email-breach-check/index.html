Email Breach Check

Check whether an email address has appeared in known data breaches.

Check whether an email address appears in known data breaches, using the free XposedOrNot service. If it does, change that account's password and turn on two-factor authentication.

Privacy note: the email address is sent to XposedOrNot (api.xposedornot.com) to run the check — that is how the service works. It is not sent to Cheakstar, and Cheakstar keeps no record of it. A clean result means "not in the breaches this service knows about", not a guarantee for all time.
