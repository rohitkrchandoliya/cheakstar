Image Tools

Compress, convert, or resize your photos. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Image Compressor

Shrink a JPG, PNG, or WebP image to a smaller file size, for example to fit a form upload limit.

Lower quality means a smaller file but a softer image. PNG output ignores the quality setting (PNG is lossless), so pick JPEG or WebP for the smallest files. Target-size mode tries several quality levels and keeps the best fit; very small targets may not be reachable without shrinking the image, and the page will say so.

Image Converter

Convert an image between JPG, PNG, and WebP. The file never leaves your device.

Converting to PNG from a photo usually makes a larger file, because PNG is lossless. Converting to JPEG drops transparency: transparent areas become white. HEIC photos from iPhones are not supported in this version.

Image Resizer & Cropper

Resize an image to exact pixels, or crop it to an aspect ratio. Includes passport-photo size presets.

Resizing to a larger size than the original will not add detail; it only stretches the image. Passport sizes here use 300 dpi equivalents (2 x 2 inch = 600 x 600 px, 35 x 45 mm = 413 x 531 px). Always check the official photo rules of the form or authority you are submitting to.

Privacy: your images are processed only in this browser tab and are never sent to any server. Closing this page removes them.
