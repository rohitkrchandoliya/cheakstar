Countdown Timer

Count down the days, hours and minutes to any date or event.

Counting down to an exam, a wedding, a launch or a holiday? Name the event, pick the date, and watch the time left. The link in your address bar updates too — copy it to share this exact countdown with anyone.

–Days
–Hours
–Minutes
–Seconds

Good to know: the event name and date travel inside the link itself (the part after the #), so sharing works without any account or server — and that part of a link is never sent to the website when the page loads. If the date has already passed, the timer says so instead of counting into negative numbers.
