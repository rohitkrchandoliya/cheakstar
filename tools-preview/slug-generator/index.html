Slug Generator

Turn a title into a clean, lowercase URL slug.

Type a blog title or page heading and get a tidy URL slug — lowercase, words joined with hyphens, accents removed. For example, "How to Make the Best Coffee at Home!" becomes how-to-make-the-best-coffee-at-home.

Good to know: a slug is the readable part of a page address — it helps readers and search engines understand what the page is about. Accented letters are converted to plain ones (é becomes e), and anything that is not a letter or a number is replaced with the separator. Everything runs in this browser tab; your title is never sent anywhere.
