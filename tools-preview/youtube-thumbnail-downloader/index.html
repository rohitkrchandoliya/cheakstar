YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

Get the public thumbnail of any YouTube video in HD or SD.

Paste a YouTube video link or video ID. Thumbnails are public image URLs served by YouTube itself, so downloading them is fine for any public video.

Guide: download your own YouTube videos, legally.

This tool cannot download videos. Downloading other people's videos breaks YouTube's Terms of Service, so no video-download feature is offered here. To get your own videos legally:

  1. Your own uploads: open YouTube Studio, go to Content, open the video's menu (three dots), and choose Download. YouTube gives you the original file you uploaded.
  2. Watch offline: with a YouTube Premium subscription, the YouTube app lets you save videos to watch offline inside the app.

Thumbnails are a different case: they are public images served by YouTube itself, which is why this downloader works for any public video. The thumbnail images load directly from YouTube's image servers; no data is sent to Cheakstar.
