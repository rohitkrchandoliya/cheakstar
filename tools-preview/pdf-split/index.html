PDF Split — Extract & Delete Pages

Extract chosen pages from a PDF, or delete pages from it.

Split a PDF by taking pages out of it. Add a PDF, type the pages you want (for example 1,3-5), then extract just those pages as a new PDF — or delete those pages and keep the rest.

Your files stay on your device. Splitting runs entirely in your browser using the open-source pdf-lib library (MIT licence). Nothing is uploaded to any server. The original file is never changed — you always download a new PDF.
