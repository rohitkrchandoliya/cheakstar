Random Number Generator

Generate random numbers in any range, with or without repeats.

For draws, classroom picks, raffles, testing data or breaking a tie. Set the smallest and largest numbers allowed, how many you want, and whether the same number may appear twice.

Good to know: the numbers come from your device's cryptographic random generator — the same source browsers use for security work — and a technique called rejection sampling keeps every number in the range equally likely, with no hidden favour toward small numbers. Ordinary generators used in games and scripts are predictable by comparison.
