Barcode Generator

Create downloadable barcodes from your text or numbers.

Type the text or number you want encoded, pick a barcode format, and generate a barcode you can download as a PNG or SVG file. EAN-13 and UPC barcodes follow the shop standard, including the check digit at the end, so the number under the bars always matches what a scanner reads.

Good to know: barcodes are generated on your device, in this browser tab — your text is never sent to any server; only the small barcode library is loaded from a public CDN. For EAN-13 you may type the first 12 digits and the tool adds the correct check digit as the 13th; if you type all 13, the check digit is verified and a wrong one is reported instead of drawing a barcode a scanner would reject. UPC works the same way with 11 or 12 digits. Print size matters for real-world scanning: keep quiet (blank) space around the bars and print at a reasonable size.
