Image Watermark

Stamp your name, brand or logo onto an image before you share it — text or logo, placed once or tiled across the whole picture.

Drop an image, choose a text watermark or your own logo, set its size, opacity and position, and download the result. The preview updates live. Your image is processed on this device only and never leaves this browser tab.

Watermark

Download watermarked image

Choose or drop an image to watermark it.

Good to know — please read: a visible watermark deters casual reuse and shows ownership at a glance. It is not copy protection: a watermark can be cropped out or edited away by someone determined, and it does not stop anyone downloading the file. For work that must stay private, do not publish it — no watermark changes that. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is sent anywhere.
