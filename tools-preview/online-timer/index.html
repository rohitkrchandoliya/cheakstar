Online Timer

A countdown timer with presets and an audible alarm.

Set a countdown for cooking, exercise, study sessions or classroom activities. Pick a preset or type your own time — when the countdown finishes, an alarm sounds on this device.

Good to know: the countdown is worked out from the clock, not by counting screen updates, so it stays accurate even if you switch to another tab. Keep this tab open and your sound on — the alarm plays in this page, and on some phones the screen must stay awake for the sound to be heard the moment the time is up. Where the browser supports it, the timer asks the screen to stay awake while it runs.
