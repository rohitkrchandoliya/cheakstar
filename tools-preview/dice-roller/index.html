Dice Roller

Roll one to six dice, from a d4 up to a d20.

For board games, classroom activities and tabletop role-playing games. Choose the kind of die, choose how many to roll, and see each die and the total.

Good to know: every roll uses your device's cryptographic random generator with rejection sampling, so each face is equally likely and rolls are independent — a die has no memory of what it showed last time. The history below keeps your last 10 rolls on this page only.
