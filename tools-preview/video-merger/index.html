Video Merger

Join two or more small videos into one MP4, in your chosen order.

Honest limits, so there are no surprises: the media engine (ffmpeg) runs inside your browser and loads once (about 20–31 MB from a public CDN, then it is cached). Keep the combined size under about 50 MB and use short clips; any single file over 100 MB is refused. Clips are re-encoded to one size (the first clip's size), so clips recorded on different devices still join cleanly. The engine is a single-thread build, so merging is slower than a desktop app. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.
