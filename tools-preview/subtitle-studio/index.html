Subtitle Studio

Write and edit subtitles in your browser — fix timings, spot mistakes, and export clean SRT or WebVTT files.

Load a subtitle file (SRT or WebVTT) — or start from an empty list — and edit every cue's start, end and text in the table. Optionally load the video too: clicking a cue jumps the video to that moment. Problems such as overlapping cues, an end before the start, or empty text are flagged on the row as you type, before they can break a player. Everything happens on this device — your video and subtitles never leave this browser tab.

#StartEndTextCheck

Load a subtitle file, or press “Start with an empty cue list” below.

Good to know — please read: times are edited as HH:MM:SS.mmm; when you load a file, SRT comma decimals are understood and converted. On export, cues are written in start-time order and numbered from 1 — if a cue still has a timing problem the export is blocked and the row tells you what to fix, rather than quietly writing a broken file. Splitting a cue divides it at its midpoint and puts the second half of the text in the new cue when the text has more than one word. Styling tags inside subtitle text are kept as plain text. Nothing is uploaded.
