Webcam Test

See your camera preview and check it before a call.

Start your camera and see the live picture before you join a meeting or a class, so there are no surprises on the call. The page also reads back the real resolution and frame rate your camera is sending, which is what the other side actually sees.

Press Start camera to begin. The browser will ask for camera permission.

Good to know: the preview is local only — no image or video leaves this device, and nothing is recorded or uploaded. The only picture that ever exists is the PNG you choose to save with the Snapshot button. Resolution and frame rate are read from the camera itself; some cameras do not report a frame rate or facing mode, and the page says so instead of guessing.
