Audio Converter

Convert audio between MP3, WAV, M4A and OGG, with trim, speed and loudness options.

Honest limits, so there are no surprises: the media engine (ffmpeg) runs inside your browser and loads once (about 20–31 MB from a public CDN, then it is cached). Files up to about 50 MB work best; files over 100 MB are refused because browser memory is limited. The engine is a single-thread build, so converting is slower than a desktop app (about 10–20% of native speed). Your files are never uploaded anywhere.
