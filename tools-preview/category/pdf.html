PDF Tools

Eight tools for the PDF jobs that come up in study and office work. Combine several PDFs into one in your chosen order, extract or delete pages, turn images into a single PDF, render a PDF's pages back into images, make a PDF smaller, rotate pages that scanned in sideways, pull the text out of a PDF as a plain text file, or turn typed text into a clean PDF. Every job runs on this device, so documents you would not email to a stranger never leave your browser. Handy for students assembling assignments, applicants preparing documents for portals, and anyone tidying paperwork before sending it.
