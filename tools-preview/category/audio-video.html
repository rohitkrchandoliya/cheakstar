Audio & Video Tools

Twelve tools for quick audio and video jobs on short clips, handled inside the browser. Convert audio between MP3, WAV, M4A and OGG or video between MP4 and WebM, pull the sound out of a video as an MP3, trim a clip, join files together, make a video smaller, change its speed, or turn its first seconds into an animated GIF. You can also inspect a media file's duration, resolution and codecs, and record your voice or your screen and download the result. Suited to students, new creators and anyone who needs one small clip fixed without installing editing software. Three device-test tools help you check your setup before a call: a microphone test with a live level meter and playback, a webcam test with a resolution readout, and a speaker and headphone test with left and right channel sounds.
