Image Tools

Eight tools for preparing pictures without uploading them to a stranger's server. Inspect an image's size, dimensions and stored camera details (EXIF), shrink it to a smaller file, convert between JPG, PNG and WebP, resize or crop it to exact pixels, and make a passport photo at 35 x 45 mm with a printable sheet of copies. There is also a tool that shrinks an image to an exact size in KB for online form uploads, a colour picker that reads the HEX and RGB value of any point in a picture, and a favicon generator for websites. Made for students and applicants filling in online forms, website owners, and anyone who needs a photo to meet a stated size or format.
