QR & Video Links

Four small tools for the codes and links people share every day. Create a QR code for a link, a block of text, a Wi-Fi network, a UPI ID or a contact card, or read a QR code from an image to see what it really contains before you act on it. For video links, fetch a YouTube thumbnail in HD or SD, or check a YouTube or Vimeo link's title, channel and thumbnail before you share it on. Useful for teachers, shopkeepers and creators who print codes on posters and cards, and for anyone who wants to see where a code or a video link actually leads.
