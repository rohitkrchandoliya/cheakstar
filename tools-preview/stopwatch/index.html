Stopwatch

A precision stopwatch with laps, best and worst lap highlights.

Time anything — a run, a recipe step, a classroom activity. The stopwatch keeps running while this page stays open, and laps are recorded to a hundredth of a second.

00:00.00

Good to know: timing is measured with your device's high-resolution clock, accurate to about a hundredth of a second as shown. If you switch to another tab the stopwatch keeps counting correctly, although the display updates less often while the tab is in the background. Closing or reloading the page clears the time and the laps — copy the results first if you need them.
