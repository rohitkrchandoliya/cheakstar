Domain Info (WHOIS / RDAP)

See a domain's registrar, creation and expiry dates, nameservers and status.

This is the modern RDAP version of a WHOIS lookup: the record comes from the registry for that domain ending, via rdap.org.

Domain information

Notes: the lookup uses the public RDAP service (rdap.org, which redirects to the registry for that domain ending). Owner names and emails are hidden by most registries for privacy — that is normal, and this tool cannot reveal them. Nothing is sent to Cheakstar.
