Speaker / Headphone Test

Play test sounds to check your speakers or headphones.

Check that your speakers or headphones are working before a call or a class. Play a plain tone, test the left and right sides one at a time, or run a sweep that climbs from a low note to a high one — each sound is made in your browser at the moment you press the button.

Pick a test sound. Keep the volume modest the first time.

Good to know: every sound is generated in your browser with the Web Audio API — no audio files are downloaded and nothing leaves your device. If you hear the left-channel sound on your right side (or the other way round), your speakers or headphones are swapped or reversed, or a mono setting somewhere is mixing the two channels together.
