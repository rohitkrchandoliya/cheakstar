Artwork Enhancer

Give flat digital artwork the grain of traditional media — a colour-noise overlay, the classic trick digital painters use to make an image feel organic.

Perfectly flat digital colour can look sterile. Painters have long fixed that by laying a layer of random colour noise over the artwork in overlay blend mode: every pixel gets a tiny random push toward a different colour, so flat areas gain the subtle, living texture of paper, canvas or film grain. The classic recipe uses just 2% opacity — enough to feel, not enough to see. Your image is processed on this device only and never leaves this browser tab.

Opacity is how strong the grain is (2% is the classic subtle recipe; push it up to see the effect clearly). Scale is the size of each noise block — 1 is fine film grain, higher values are chunkier, more painterly blocks.

Good to know: the noise is generated fresh for your image in the browser — every pixel block gets a fully random colour, drawn enlarged so blocks stay crisp, then blended in overlay mode at your chosen opacity. Images are worked at up to 2048 px on the longest side (a very large original is scaled down first, which is also the size of the download) so the grain stays fast even on a phone. Moving a slider keeps the same grain pattern so you can judge strength fairly; “New grain” deals a fresh pattern. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is sent anywhere.
