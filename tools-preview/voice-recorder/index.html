Voice Recorder

Record voice memos from your microphone and download them. Every memo is kept in a takes list, so you can record several and keep the best.

The recording never leaves this device — it is made and stored only in this browser tab. Use it for a voice note, a song idea or a quick dictation. Each finished memo is added to the takes list under the player; the newest one also appears in the player above.

Ready. Your browser will ask for microphone permission when you start.
