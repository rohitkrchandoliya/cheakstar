FD Calculator

Estimate the maturity value and interest of a fixed deposit.

Indian banks compound fixed deposit interest every quarter. Enter your deposit, the bank's rate and the tenure to estimate what you will receive at maturity.

Honest note: most Indian banks add about 0.50% for senior citizens, which is the extra this page applies when you tick the box — check your bank's actual senior rate. Interest on an FD is taxable, and banks deduct TDS when the interest crosses the yearly limit. The maturity figure is an estimate before tax.
