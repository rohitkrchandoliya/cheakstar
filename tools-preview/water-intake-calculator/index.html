Water Intake Calculator

Estimate how much water you should drink in a day.

Your water need depends mostly on your body weight, how active you are, and the weather. Enter those three things for a practical daily target in litres and glasses.

General guidance only: this uses the widely used guide of about 35 ml of water per kilogram of body weight, plus extra for activity and heat. Food, tea and other drinks also contribute water. People with kidney, heart or other medical conditions, and those who are pregnant, should follow their doctor's advice on fluid intake instead of a general calculator.
