Video Link Info

See a YouTube or Vimeo video's title, channel and thumbnail from its link.

This uses the free oEmbed service (noembed as a fallback). It does not download the video.

Privacy note: the lookup contacts YouTube's or Vimeo's public oEmbed service (or noembed), and the thumbnail loads from that platform's image servers. No data is sent to Cheakstar.
