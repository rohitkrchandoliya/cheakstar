Tailwind Shade Generator

Turn one base colour into a full 50–950 shade scale, with CSS variable and Tailwind config exports.

Pick a base colour and name your palette. The scale is built in OKLCH, the perceptual colour space Tailwind v4 uses, so the steps look evenly spaced instead of bunching up at one end. Click any swatch to copy its HEX code.

Good to know: step 500 is your base colour exactly. Lighter steps move the OKLCH lightness toward 0.985 and darker steps toward 0.16, with chroma gently tapered at both ends (real pigments get less vivid near white and black); the hue never changes. If a step would fall outside the sRGB gamut, its chroma is reduced just enough to fit, so every value here is a real displayable colour. Everything runs in this browser tab; nothing is sent anywhere.
