Guide: download your own YouTube videos, legally

This page cannot download videos. Downloading other people's videos breaks YouTube's Terms of Service, so no video-download feature is offered here. To get your own videos legally:

  1. Your own uploads: open YouTube Studio, go to Content, open the video's menu (three dots), and choose Download. YouTube gives you the original file you uploaded.
  2. Watch offline: with a YouTube Premium subscription, the YouTube app lets you save videos to watch offline inside the app.

Thumbnails are a different case: they are public images served by YouTube itself, which is why the downloader above works for any public video.

Privacy note: the thumbnail images load directly from YouTube's image servers, and the video-info lookup contacts YouTube's or Vimeo's public oEmbed service (or noembed). No data is sent to Cheakstar.
