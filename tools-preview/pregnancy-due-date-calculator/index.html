Pregnancy Due Date Calculator

Estimate your due date and current week from your last period date.

Enter the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). The calculator applies Naegele's rule — the standard estimate doctors start from — to give your expected due date and how far along the pregnancy is today.

Estimate only — confirm with your doctor: Naegele's rule assumes a regular 28-day cycle and counts 280 days from the last period. Only about 1 in 20 babies arrive on the exact due date; a first ultrasound usually gives a more accurate date. This calculator does not store or send the date you enter.
