Auto Subtitle

Turn speech into subtitles with OpenAI's Whisper AI, running entirely in your browser — the recording never leaves your device.

Drop an audio or video file, pick a model and a language, and get a subtitle file (SRT and WebVTT) made by Whisper, transcribed on this device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Please read before the first run: the model you pick is downloaded once from Hugging Face (the runtime library, ~24 MB, comes from the jsDelivr CDN at the same time) and then cached by your browser, so later visits can work offline. Tiny is about 43 MB and fast but rough; Base is about 77 MB, slower, and noticeably more accurate. Honest expectations: clear speech in quiet conditions transcribes well; background music, heavy noise, overlapping speakers and strong accents will produce mistakes, and names are often misheard. Transcription runs on your CPU, so it is not fast — expect it to take a good fraction of the recording's length, and several times longer than the recording on a slow machine. Short clips are the sweet spot.
